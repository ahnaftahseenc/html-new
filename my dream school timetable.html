School Subject Descriptions

Mathematics
A subject that deals with numbers, quantities, and shapes. It helps develop logical thinking and problem-solving skills.
Science
A subject that studies the natural world, space and the laws that govern it. It helps develop critical thinking and an understanding of how things work.
English
A subject that focuses on reading, writing, and communication skills. It helps develop language proficiency and the ability to express ideas clearly.
Computer Science
A subject that focuses on programming, algorithms, and computational thinking. It helps develop problem-solving skills and an understanding of how computers work.
Entrepreneurship
A subject that teaches the fundamentals of starting and running a business. It helps develop critical thinking and an understanding of how to create and manage a successful venture.
Art
A subject that focuses on creative expression and the appreciation of visual arts. It helps develop imagination and the ability to communicate ideas through various artistic mediums.
